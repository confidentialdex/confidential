# Roadmap

Welcome to the Confidential DEX Roadmap! Our vision is to build the ultimate universal leverage layer on the Arc Network, providing zero borrow fees and deep liquidity through our dual-tranche vaults. 

Below is an outline of our development phases. Please note that this roadmap is a living document and may evolve based on community feedback and market conditions.

---

## 🚀 Phase 1: Community & Beta Testnet (Q3 2026)
*Establishing our presence and initiating our first public beta tests.*

- **Community & Brand:** 
  - Official launch of the Confidential DEX X (Twitter) community and social channels.
  - Deployment of the official Landing Page to introduce our vision, features, and Arc Network integration.
- **Technical Plan:** 
  - Roll out the Beta Testnet to gather early feedback from the community and power users.
  - Stress-test early iterations of the matching engine and user interface (UI).

---

## 🟢 Phase 2: Security, Arc Testnet Hardening & Opt-In Privacy (Q4 2026)
*Laying the secure foundation, integrating LP privacy, and optimizing for the Arc Network ecosystem while the Public Testnet remains running.*

- **Technical Plan:** 
  - Upgrade our vault infrastructure to support confidential, zero-knowledge based deposits and withdrawals for institutional players.
  - Complete rigorous internal stress-testing of our emergency Auto-Deleveraging (ADL) thresholds.
  - Conduct a comprehensive third-party security audit of the `ConfidentialTradingV1` and `ConfidentialVaultV1` smart contracts (including privacy features).
  - Maximize the building and execution reliability of our custom Unified Keeper network.
- **Circle/Arc Integration:** 
  - Direct integration with Arc's upcoming native opt-in privacy features, enabling shielded LP deposits to protect liquidity movements on-chain.
  - Continued optimization on the **Public Arc Testnet**, focusing on maximizing gas efficiency for the Unified Keeper network.
  - Ensure flawless compliance with native USDC token standards before mainnet deployment.

---

## 🟡 Phase 3: Arc Mainnet Launch & Liquidity Bootstrapping (Q1 2027)
*The official launch of Confidential DEX and our public liquidity vaults.*

- **Technical Plan:** 
  - Deploy the fully audited Confidential DEX smart contracts.
  - Launch the live React frontend.
  - Open the Dual-Tranche (Degen & Prime) Vaults to the public.
  - Launch the Points Farming (Farm Point) program to reward early user participation and engagement.
- **Circle/Arc Integration:** 
  - Official deployment natively on the Arc Mainnet. 
  - Bootstrap initial TVL using **USDC** as the exclusive collateral, margin, and settlement asset for all trading activities and vault deposits.

---

## 🔵 Phase 4: Community Incentives & Growth Campaign (Q2 2027)
*Driving user acquisition through sustainable on-chain incentives.*

- **Technical Plan:** 
  - Develop and deploy closed-loop "Promotional Trading Coupon" smart contracts.
  - Sustainably fund these coupons by redirecting 1% of liquidation penalties to drive user acquisition.
- **Circle/Arc Integration:** 
  - Leverage native USDC utility to fund, distribute, and settle trading coupons entirely on-chain.
  - Drive organic trading volume and deeper ecosystem engagement on the Arc network.
